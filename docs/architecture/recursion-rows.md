# Recursion rows

D10 of [inline cost](../../crates/opt/llrm-transforms/src/inlinecost.rs): a tree-recursive function is copied into itself as gcc's [`recursive_inlining`](https://github.com/gcc-mirror/gcc/blob/releases/gcc-13.3.0/gcc/ipa-inline.cc#L1645) does, to depth 3 (gcc: 8), 150 `gcc_insns` (gcc: 450, [params.opt](https://github.com/gcc-mirror/gcc/blob/releases/gcc-13.3.0/gcc/params.opt#L549)) and the call's frequency over the entry's above 10% ([`want_inline_self_recursive_call_p`](https://github.com/gcc-mirror/gcc/blob/releases/gcc-13.3.0/gcc/ipa-inline.cc#L991), [L1067](https://github.com/gcc-mirror/gcc/blob/releases/gcc-13.3.0/gcc/ipa-inline.cc#L1067)), only where its distinct self-calls average one or more per call (a tree), not at -Os. Measured with the installed gcc 13.4.0 binary's `-Q --help=params`; the cited source is 13.3.0 (same defaults).

Where it runs, and why there: the pin runs the function specializer inside IPSCCP, before its inliner ([PassBuilderPipelines.cpp:1228](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/d1106deb71cc81016406a1917d0508d2df296266/llvm/lib/Passes/PassBuilderPipelines.cpp#L1228), the inliner pipeline at [L1331](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/d1106deb71cc81016406a1917d0508d2df296266/llvm/lib/Passes/PassBuilderPipelines.cpp#L1331)), and tail-call elimination in the function simplification pipeline inside it ([L688](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/d1106deb71cc81016406a1917d0508d2df296266/llvm/lib/Passes/PassBuilderPipelines.cpp#L688)). Here ipa-cp's second round is the interprocedural step after the walk, so the copies made in the walk enlarged `chop` before ipa-cp priced its clones (recchop -O3: 4 defines against 12, 6368 clocks against 6209). DEVIATE from the pin's TRE slot: a tree-recursive function is marked in the walk (`tre-deferred`), skips tail-call elimination there, and gets its copies and then its elimination at the end of the interprocedural step (`walk::SelfInline`). Linear recursions keep the pin's order (a tail-recursive callee is a loop before its caller inlines it, #3256).

Cleanup (D10, third part; DEVIATE from the pin; owner w-6; exit: remove the second Simplify run when D10's admission can run the cleanup in the pin's inline-then-cleanup order, or when the pin-faithful order gives fib/x_fib at or below main's 227171 without it; review 2026-11-30: re-measure fib/x_fib/rectwo clocks, and if neither exit is reachable it becomes a permanent numbered DEVIATE with this evidence restated): a body that got copies runs the Simplify list twice (`walk::SelfInline`). The pin has no such second run: its function simplification pipeline runs once on a function in the CGSCC walk (`NoRerun`, [PassBuilderPipelines.cpp:1047](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/d1106deb71cc81016406a1917d0508d2df296266/llvm/lib/Passes/PassBuilderPipelines.cpp#L1047)), and it never has self-copies to clean (LLVM refuses a recursive callee). gcc cleans the copies with the passes after `pass_ipa_inline` (FRE and PRE run again, `passes.def`). Here the list runs GVN before jump threading, and the copies' paths are joined by the threading, so run once each copy repeated the calls of the copies that dominate it (fib -O2, depth 3: 154398 clocks, 1426 spills, 12 calls; run twice 14802 clocks, 0 spills). Rows pair at -O1/-O2/-O3/-Os: fib and x_fib -O2/-O3 154398 to 14802, frames -15.6%, rectwo -0.3%, nothing else changes, QCport identical. Answers: the rows' fib, x_fib and frames print the same values as gcc and clang at every level (6765, 6765, 616371). fib -O2 is 14802 clocks against gcc's 210636 and clang's 362808: the calls are pure, and sharing them across the copies makes the recursion cheaper than a tree walk, which neither compiler does.

Differences from gcc: the copies are made depth first, gcc's by a heap of edges ordered by badness; gcc's `peeling` and cold-edge checks are not translated.

Peeling (D10, second half): a recursive callee inlined into another function has its own call inlined in the copy, as gcc's [`inline_small_functions`](https://github.com/gcc-mirror/gcc/blob/releases/gcc-13.3.0/gcc/ipa-inline.cc#L2237) does for an edge whose caller is a copy of the callee. [`want_inline_self_recursive_call_p`](https://github.com/gcc-mirror/gcc/blob/releases/gcc-13.3.0/gcc/ipa-inline.cc#L1033) compares the edge's frequency with that of the call that made the outermost copy, which is the calls to itself per call to the power of the copies deep, with 1 - 1/8 squared once for each copy before it where the body has more than one call to itself (DEVIATE: their copies grow as the calls to the power of the depth, which the squaring bounds; with one call they grow by one a level, and without the squaring hanoi goes from 133 to 103 clocks, 152 to 184 bytes, main 96 / 125, and no row is worse than with it); here the copy is peeled while that is below the limit, a copy adds at most `max-inline-insns-auto` (15) `gcc_insns`, and the copies of one call add at most 450, gcc's recursive growth limit (DEVIATE: gcc has no such cap on peeling; two calls in exclusive branches double the copies at each level, recpow -O1 32 copies and 8203 bytes); not at -Os. DEVIATE: gcc reads the one edge, here the function's calls to itself per call, so a tree (fib 1, bintree 1.25) is never peeled (peeling every edge doubled bintree's copy at each level: -O2 77666 against 77564 clocks, 988 to 1276 bytes).

## DEVIATE REC-1: recursive inlining is a flag, off by default

The pin's inliner does not copy a callee that calls itself into its caller (`IsRecursiveCall`, `AllowRecursiveCall` off); llrm's default now refuses it too (REC-2). gcc's `recursive_inlining` and peeling (above) copy it into itself and into its callers, nine levels deep, which is not the pin's behaviour: hanoi's two identical calls became one by CSE, then `bench_hanoi` took nine copies of `hanoi` and a lookup table for `n < 9`, 40 instructions where gcc runs 35,764 and the pin's clang 249,852 (the 2026-10-23 bench table). The three mechanisms (copies of a function in itself, peeling of a recursive callee's own call in the copy, and the deferral of its tail-call elimination until the copies are made) are `-finline-recursive` (`Threshold::recursive`), off by default; `walk::tests::test_the_default_does_not_copy_a_recursive_callee` and `test_only_a_tree_recursion_waits_for_its_tail_call_elimination` pin the default. Owner: w-8 (flag); w-6 owns the rows above. Exit: the pin-faithful order of inlining and cleanup reaches the rows above without the flag, or the user turns it on.

Not covered by the flag: nothing. A call with a constant argument was inlined again on later rounds only because the callee itself was inlined once and its loop unrolled; the callee is refused now (REC-2).

REC-1 update (2026-10-31, w9): the real-mode target takes a bounded recursion without the flag, at speed only: `Tti::recursive_inlining` (m16: depth 2, a recursive body of at most 14 `gcc_insns`, the 150 `gcc_insns` growth budget and the peeling limits unchanged), so the walk prices a recursive callee (`AllowRecursiveCall`, pin: [`IsRecursiveCall`](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/d1106deb71cc81016406a1917d0508d2df296266/llvm/lib/Analysis/InlineCost.cpp#L2551)) only up to that size and `SelfInline` copies it to that depth. The cap is gcc's `max-inline-insns-auto` 15 ([params.opt:545](https://github.com/gcc-mirror/gcc/blob/releases/gcc-13.3.0/gcc/params.opt#L545)) less one: bintree's body is 15 and its copies grow it 273 to 702 B. Measured m16 -O2 i386 (clocks/bytes; main 854bb7efc4): hanoi 199/37 to 92/71 (107/113), fib 356059/60 to 17585/152 (298686/282), rectwo 2382380/80 to 326601/259 (1765045/421), frames 9280596/169 to 6433661/338 (8807338/430); depth 3 or a cap of 16 or more: rectwo 540 B, bintree 956 B. Bounds: the copies need inlining on (nib and BASIC pass `-fno-inline-functions`: frames and fib nib stay 186 and 63 B, as gcc's early inliner takes no uninlined-declared call without it), and the cap counts a body's loops at ten trips (`gcc_work`; a body above it is not copied and keeps its tail-call elimination at the pin's slot: recchop 10242 clocks, 11310 with the deferral). The four rows above are -O2 byte rows, each below main, with clocks at or past it. Exit: the user's decision on REC-1, or the pin-faithful order reaching these rows.

## Tail-recursion elimination at m16 -Os (w9, 2026-10-26)

`recchop` is 158 B on main, 169 B on parity `19a593cf2`, 153 B with `-fno-optimize-sibling-calls`: the pin's `TailCallElim` makes the last `chop` a loop and three values (`lo`, `mid`, the sum) cross the other call. Over the whole c/nib/qb corpus at -Os m16 (`sz4.py`, with and without the flag): the pass shrinks 33 program variants by 1725 B and grows 24 by 390 B, net -1335 B. Its growers are `recchop` (+16), `idiv_loop_os` (+27), `flat_arith` (+22), `tail_calls` (+13), `frames` (+7), `rectwo`, `fib`, `quicksort.nib`; no single property separates them from the winners (`bintree`, `recsum`, `recgcd`, `recfloat`, `quicksort.c`). A gate on the pass would give up the wins; the growth is spill code in the loop it makes, which the allocation rows own. Not a DEVIATE.
